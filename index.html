01

Input, Arithmetic & Decisions

Read values, calculate results, and make decisions.

# Problem Main Learning What to Do
01 Simple CalculatorSheet 1 · C input(), int, split(), map(), unpacking, arithmetic, and formatted output. Read two numbers and print their sum, product, and difference in the exact requested format.
02 Digits SummationSheet 1 · F Modulo % and identifying the information actually needed. Extract the last digit of each of two numbers and print the sum of those two digits.
03 WatermelonSheet 8 · B if/else, and, parity, and boundary cases. Determine whether a watermelon can be split into two positive, even-weight parts. Check the special case of weight 2.
04 Age in DaysSheet 1 · R Integer division //, remainder %, and sequential calculations. Convert an age in days into years, months, and remaining days, using 365 days per year and 30 days per month.
05 Area of a CircleSheet 1 · E float, exponentiation **, and decimal formatting. Calculate a circle's area using the statement's value of π and print exactly nine digits after the decimal point.
02

Loops: Repeat, Filter & Simulate

Understand what repeats, what changes, and when to stop.

# Problem Main Learning What to Do
06 1 to NSheet 2 · A for, range(), indentation, and exclusive range endpoints. Print all integers from 1 through N, each on a separate line.
07 Even NumbersSheet 2 · B Combining loops with conditions, filtering, and range steps. Print the even numbers from 1 through N. Print -1 if there are none.
08 Bear and Big BrotherSheet 8 · D while, simulation, counters, and stopping conditions. Simulate annual weight growth and count the years until the smaller bear becomes strictly heavier than the larger bear.
09 FactorialSheet 2 · G Product accumulators, multiple test cases, and resetting state. Calculate the factorial for each test case. Start the product at 1 and handle 0! = 1.
03

Lists: Store, Traverse, Search & Build

Work with ordered collections and preserve the information that matters.

# Problem Main Learning What to Do
10 SummationSheet 3 · A list(map(...)), traversal, accumulation, sum(), and abs(). Print the absolute value of the array's total sum, not the sum of the absolute values of individual elements.
11 Even, Odd, Positive and NegativeSheet 2 · C Multiple counters, if/elif/else, and independent classifications. Count even, odd, positive, and negative values. Zero is even but neither positive nor negative.
12 SearchingSheet 3 · B Indexing, enumerate(), linear search, break, and default answers. Print the zero-based index of the target's first occurrence, or -1 if it is absent.
13 Shift ZerosSheet 5 · N append(), list construction, def, parameters, return, and unpacked printing. Use a function to move every zero to the end while preserving the original order of non-zero elements.
04

Strings: Inspect, Transform & Reassemble

Apply familiar sequence operations to text.

# Problem Main Learning What to Do
14 Way Too Long WordsSheet 4 · F len(), first/last characters, negative indexing, and string formatting. Abbreviate words longer than 10 characters using the first letter, the number of middle characters, and the last letter.
15 PalindromeSheet 4 · I Symmetric comparisons, string equality, and reverse slicing [::-1]. Determine whether the string reads the same forward and backward.
16 Helpful MathsSheet 8 · T split("+"), sorted(), and "+".join(...). Rearrange the operands of an addition expression into non-decreasing order. Operands are the single digits 1, 2, and 3.
05

Sets & Dictionaries: Choose the Right Tool

Represent uniqueness, membership, frequencies, and key-value relationships.

# Problem Main Learning What to Do
17 Distinct NumbersSheet 5 · M set(), uniqueness, len(), and functions returning values. Use a function to count the distinct numbers in an array. The array may be empty.
18 Good NumberSheet 8 · S Character sets, membership with in, and subset checking. Count numbers containing every digit from 0 through k at least once. Additional digits are allowed.
19 FootballSheet 9 · W dict, .get(), frequency counting, and iterating over .items(). Build a scoreboard from the team name recorded for each goal, then print the winning team. A tie is not possible.
20 Frequency ArraySheet 3 · V Frequency lists versus dictionaries, bounded keys, and missing-value counts. Count occurrences and print a count for every value from 1 through M, including values that never appear.
21 Encrypt & Decrypt MessageSheet 4 · W Character-to-character dictionaries, zip(), reverse mappings, and join(). Encrypt or decrypt a message by translating each character with the substitution key supplied in the statement.
06

Basic Maths & Efficiency

Use number properties and constraints to avoid unnecessary work.

# Problem Main Learning What to Do
22 Summation from 1 to NSheet 1 · G Loop versus formula, integer arithmetic, and time-complexity intuition. Find 1 + 2 + … + N for N up to one billion. Derive a formula instead of performing N iterations.
23 DivisorsSheet 2 · K Divisibility, generating candidates, and filtering with modulo. Print all positive divisors of a number in ascending order, one per line.
24 One PrimeSheet 2 · H Early exit, factor pairs, and checking divisors only up to the square root. Determine whether a number is prime. Explain why a composite number must have a factor no larger than its square root.
25 GCDSheet 6 · H Euclid's algorithm, while, simultaneous assignment, and math.gcd(). Print both the greatest common divisor and least common multiple of two positive integers.
07

Application Challenges: Combine Your Tools

Bring familiar language features together in games, grids, and simulations.

# Problem Main Learning What to Do
26 Fox And SnakeSheet 8 · F Row patterns, parity, string repetition, and output construction. Print a snake pattern on a grid, alternating full rows with connecting rows at the appropriate edge.
27 MarksSheet 10 · F Rows and columns, nested loops, maxima, and a set of student indices. Count students who have the highest mark in at least one subject. Include tied toppers and count each student only once.
28 DragonsSheet 9 · C Lists of tuples, sorting, unpacking, and greedy reasoning. Choose an order to fight all dragons, gaining strength bonuses after victories. Your strength must be strictly greater than the dragon's.
29 Sereja and DimaSheet 8 · L Two pointers, alternating turns, and simulation. Simulate two players repeatedly taking the larger of the two end cards. Print both final scores.
30 SnacktowerSheet 10 · K Set membership, add()/remove(), persistent state, and total-work analysis. Process snacks arriving in random order and place every currently available next-largest snack. Print a line for every day, even when nothing is placed.
31 BulbsSheet 10 · Q Running maximum, prefix invariants, and avoiding repeated scans. Count moments when all switched-on bulbs form a complete prefix starting at bulb 1.

This is a multi-session learning path, not a two-hour completion target. The order follows prerequisites rather than sheet numbers. The final stages are advanced relative to this beginner course.
